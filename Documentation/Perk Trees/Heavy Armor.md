# Heavy Armor
Those proficient in the usage of Heavy Armor are capable of mitigating large amounts of damage and inspire those around them.
## Rationale
In the vanilla game, there was very little reason to prefer Heavy Armor to Light Armor, as both you hit the armor cap with relative ease. Armillary changes that by reducing the efficiency of Armor Rating to 0.1% reduction per Armor Rating, and increasing the cap to 90% (total of 900 points required to cap). Furthermore, the hidden Armor Rating buff tied to simply putting on clothes/armor has been removed.

This change makes it incredibly difficult to hit the armor cap with Light Armor, even in dedicated builds, while at the same time making Heavy Armor much more competitive. Additionally, support for Paladin style builds has been included.

| :--: | :--: | :---------- |

|        | 50 | Increases armor rating for Heavy Armor by 50%, and Heavy Armor cuirasses gain 50 Armor Rating. |
| Ironclad Defenses | 25 | Each piece of equipped Heavy Armor gains 10 Armor Rating, increased to 25 if wearing a Heavy Armor cuirass. |
| Rise to Full Height | 50 | While wearing a Heavy Armor cuirass, Heavy Armor is 50% stronger. |
| Wyrd Shattering | 70 | While wearing a Heavy Armor cuirass, your Fire, Frost, and Shock resistance is increased by 25%. |
| Living Armor | 80 | While wearing a Heavy Armor cuirass, your Health Regeneration is doubled for every 400 points of Armor Rating you have. |
| Fortress | 90 | Heavy Armor cuirasses provide 100 more points of Armor Rating. |
| Conqueror | 100 | While you have 900 or more Armor Rating and wearing a Heavy Armor cuirass, you take 25% less damage from weapons. |
