# Heavy Armor
Those proficient in the usage of Heavy Armor are capable of mitigating large amounts of damage and inspire those around them.
## Mechanical Changes
"Hidden" Armor Rating modifiers are removed. The Armor Rating cap has been upped to 90%, but the cap amount has been increased to 900, for 0.1% damage mitigation per armor point. Additionally, the movement speed penalty for wearing Heavy Armor has been increased.
## Rationale
Heavy Armor has always been in a very weird spot in the vanilla game. Vanilla's relatively small Armor Rating cap of 567 (542 with a shield) means that any class can cap their armor rating with relative ease. Armillary changes the system, so that only Heavy Armor can reliably hit the armor cap, and makes it so that you are rewarded for overcapping.

Additionally, for Paladin style characters, Heavy Armors offer support options.
